# The Sunken Quarter

The Sunken Quarter was once the heart of Greyvast's wealth. Now it is a flooded scar, a district-sized wound in the earth where seawater meets mist and the boundary between street and canal has been erased entirely. The buildings that remain stand at angles that defy gravity, their foundations gutted by the impact wave, their upper floors accessible only by rope and nerve. The Tide-Weavers have mapped seven levels of descent. They have not reached the bottom.

Every morning, Tide-Runner crews lower themselves into the Quarter's depths through holes cut in what were once market square roofs. They work in teams of three: one diver, one rope-handler, one lookout for the Pale Dominion. The mist here is thicker than elsewhere — it swallows sound and distorts depth perception — and the water below is cold enough to stop a heart in minutes. The moon fragments rest in the drowned vaults of old merchant houses, cushioned by silt and the bones of those who stayed to guard their coin.

The Pale Dominion watches from the Quarter's edge. Their priest-crews have attempted three descents in the past year. Each time, the Tide-Weavers cut their ropes. Each time, the Dominion sends more priests. The Quarter does not belong to either side — it belongs to the mist, and the mist tolerates no one for long.
