# The Celestial Fracture

Two hundred years ago, on the longest night of the year, the moon shattered without warning. The event was not silent — witnesses described a sound like the world's own bones breaking, followed by a light so intense it bleached the sky white for three days. Moon fragments — some the size of keeps — plummeted into Northern Velkaris, embedding themselves in glaciers, mountains, and the seabeds of frozen fjords.

The largest fragment struck what is now Greyvast, instantly vaporizing the capital and leaving behind a crater that filled with mist and seawater. The magical aftershocks rippled outward for months, warping the fabric of reality. New forms of magic emerged — Soulmancy, Fragment-binding, Moon-tongue — but at a terrible cost. The Fracture's corruption seeps into anyone who stays too long near a fragment, eroding memory, identity, and eventually the soul.

The three entities that would become gods — Vel-Thara, Mourne, and Yssara — first manifested within the first year after the Fracture, each embodying a different aspect of the catastrophe. The old gods fell silent. The Age of Fracture began.

## Effects

- **Geography permanently altered**: moon fragments embedded across Northern Velkaris, Greyvast partially submerged in mist
- **Three new deities born**: Vel-Thara (decay), Mourne (soul-debt), Yssara (moon fragments)
- **New magic**: Soulmancy, Fragment-binding, Moon-tongue — all carrying soul-erosion cost
- **Perpetual winter**: ash and magical fallout created a permanent cooling effect
- **Soul corruption epidemic**: proximity to fragments slowly erodes memory and identity
- **Endemic despair**: Mourne's influence spreads through the emotional void left by the catastrophe
