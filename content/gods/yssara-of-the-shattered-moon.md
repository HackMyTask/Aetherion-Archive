# Yssara of the Shattered Moon

Yssara of the Shattered Moon is a primal, volatile force, less a conscious entity with defined motives and more the living embodiment of the uncontrolled, raw magic unleashed when the celestial moon shattered. She coalesced from the unbound energies of the larger fragments, a deity of pure, untamed power and cosmic chaos. Unlike the insidious Soulmancy that slowly erodes identity with each spell, Yssara represents the immediate, overwhelming surge of power available to those who dare to directly wield the moon's broken pieces. Her followers, known as the Shard-Bound, believe that by embracing the fragments, they can transcend the slow decay of Soulmancy, achieving unparalleled magical feats, often ignoring or even embracing the accelerated decay it brings to the world and their own being. Yssara does not offer solace or pacts like Mourne; she offers raw, destructive potential, a mirror to the chaos that now defines the Age of Fractured Light. Her presence is most potent in regions like Northern Velkaris, where large fragments lie embedded, twisting the landscape and drawing desperate souls to their dangerous allure. She is a force of untamed change, urging the world towards either a magnificent magical apotheosis or utter, chaotic oblivion.
