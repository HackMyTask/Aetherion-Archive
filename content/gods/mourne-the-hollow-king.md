# Mourne, the Hollow King

Mourne, the Hollow King, is not a god of ancient lineage but a chilling manifestation born from the cumulative despair and spiritual voids left by countless acts of Soulmancy. He emerged from the shadows cast by the Celestial Fracture, thriving on the erosion of identity that defines the Age of Fractured Light. Mourne embodies the ultimate price of magic, the silent, insidious debt that grows with every spell cast. He does not offer power freely, but rather promises relief from the immediate, painful cost of Soulmancy, taking a deeper, more permanent piece of the caster's soul in exchange, binding them to his will. His presence is strongest in Northern Velkaris, where desperation and isolation drive many to seek forbidden moon rituals and make pacts with unseen forces. He is a silent, calculating entity, a puppeteer of souls, collecting the hollowed fragments of those who have traded their very essence for fleeting power, and forever claiming the identities they have lost.
