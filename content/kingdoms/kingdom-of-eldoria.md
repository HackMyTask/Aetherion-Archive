The Kingdom of Eldoria is not part of this story. It is a reminder that the world continues, whole and indifferent, beyond the frozen borders of Northern Velkaris. Its fields are green. Its trade routes are open. Its people sleep through the night without fear of the Hollowed scratching at their doors. The Eldorian court watches the reports from the North with polite concern and does nothing. This is not cruelty — it is calculation. Eldoria has what the North wants (food, timber, stability) and the North has nothing Eldoria needs (ash, refugees, bad news). The rivalry with Valdenmoor is old, cold, and largely performative — a convenient excuse for both sides to maintain their isolation.
