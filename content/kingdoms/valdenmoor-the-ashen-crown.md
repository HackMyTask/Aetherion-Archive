# Valdenmoor, the Ashen Crown

Valdenmoor, the Ashen Crown, is a kingdom locked in the perpetual winter of Northern Velkaris, its ancient mountains and once-fertile valleys now choked with glacial ice and the ceaseless ashfall from the Celestial Fracture. The realm earned its moniker from the thick layer of grey dust that perpetually coats its landscapes, a grim reminder of the moon's shattering. Its people are a stoic, hardened folk, descendants of mountain clans who once thrived in isolation. Now, their culture is defined by survival against the encroaching cold and the lingering magical decay. Officially, magic is strictly forbidden, viewed as a curse that steals the soul's very essence, a price too high for a kingdom already losing so much. Yet, whispers of forbidden moon rituals persist in the shadowed valleys, practiced by desperate shamans and a growing Cult of the Broken Moon who believe salvation lies in embracing the fractured celestial power, even at the cost of their identity. The kingdom's economy, once reliant on rare mineral mining, has dwindled to subsistence hunting and the sporadic trade of unique, magically-charged ice shards found in impact craters. Led by the aging and increasingly despairing King Theron the Grim, Valdenmoor is a dying ember, clinging to its sovereignty amidst the encroaching void and the silent, hungry magic that permeates its frozen heart.
