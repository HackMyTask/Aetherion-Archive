# The Tide-Weavers

The Tide-Weavers are an independent faction of salvager-scholars who excavate the mist-choked ruins of Greyvast for valuable moon fragments. They sell to anyone — Valdenmoor's desperate lords, Pale Dominion priest-mages, Oathbound Remnant scouts — and ask no questions about how the buyer intends to use the shards. Neutrality is their trade, and survival their creed.

Their leader, Shard-Priestess Lyraea, is a pragmatic former scholar from Greyvast's royal academy who saw the fall coming and chose profit over patriotism. The Tide-Weavers operate from the Crypt of the Moonlit Depths, a partially submerged ruin on the edge of Greyvast's drowned capital that they have fortified into a trading post and research outpost.

Their salvager crews — known as "Tide-Runners" — are skilled divers, navigators of the perpetual mist, and negotiators who can out-talk or out-fight anyone.

But something has changed. In the deepest flooded chamber of Mournwater Keep, Lyraea's inner circle discovered something carved into the bedrock — symbols that predate the Celestial Fracture by centuries. They have not shared this discovery. The Tide-Weavers have stopped selling the most potent fragments from the deepest levels. The price of their usual stock has quietly doubled. They are preparing for something.
