# The Oathbound Remnants

The Oathbound Remnants are what remains of Greyvast's knightly orders, soldiers, and oath-sworn defenders who refused to die with their sunken kingdom. When the Celestial Fracture drove a moon fragment into Greyvast's capital and the Pale Dominion moved in to claim the ruins, these survivors retreated into the mist-choked wilds and swore a new oath: they would reclaim every stone of the Sunken Throne or die trying.

They are a mercenary-cult hybrid — taking paid contracts to fund their war, while simultaneously serving Mourne, the Hollow King. Their soul-sapped warriors wield the kingdom's desperation as a weapon, channeling their grief into combat prowess. Their ranks wear battered Greyvast heraldry, muted and torn, and their sigil is a broken crown half-submerged in black water.

Led by the iron-willed Commander Serevath, once captain of Greyvast's royal guard, they launch relentless guerrilla raids on Pale Dominion excavation camps, sabotage supply lines, and assassinate priest-mages who defile their ancestral lands. They are a dying cause, outnumbered and outgunned, but bound by an oath that death itself cannot dissolve.

Their ultimate goal is to drive the Pale Dominion from Greyvast entirely, even if it means every last Remnant falls in the attempt.

## Relationships

- **Greyvast, the Sunken Throne**: Fights to reclaim — The Remnants dedicate every action to reclaiming the sunken ruins of Greyvast from the Pale Dominion's excavation crews.
- **The Pale Dominion**: Resists occupation — The Remnants wage constant guerrilla war against the Pale Dominion's expansion into Greyvast.
- **Mourne, the Hollow King**: Patron deity — The Remnants venerate Mourne as their patron deity, believing their soul-debt is an honorable price for their oath.
- **Valdenmoor, the Ashen Crown**: Uneasy ally — Despite ideological differences, Valdenmoor quietly supplies the Remnants with resources, using them as proxies against the Pale Dominion.
- **The Celestial Fracture**: Forged by — The Fracture that drowned Greyvast forged the Remnants from the ashes of its knightly orders.
- **Northern Velkaris**: Operates in — The Remnants operate from hidden camps and ruins across the frozen expanse of Northern Velkaris.
