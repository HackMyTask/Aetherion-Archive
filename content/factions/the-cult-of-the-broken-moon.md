# The Cult of the Broken Moon

The Cult of the Broken Moon is a secretive and malevolent faction that operates in the frozen ruins of Northern Velkaris. They worship Yssara of the Shattered Moon as their patron deity and seek to spread their dark influence across the land. The cult is led by the enigmatic and powerful Shard-Priest Malvorn, who has mastered the forbidden moon rituals and seeks to unlock the true potential of the shattered moon. The cult's membership is shrouded in mystery, but it is known that they recruit from the desperate and the downtrodden, promising them power and salvation in exchange for their loyalty and devotion. The cult's methods are twisted and sinister, involving ritual sacrifice, soul binding, and covert infiltration. They trade with The Tide-Weavers, acquiring powerful moon fragments and forbidden knowledge in exchange for dark magic and corrupted relics. The Cult of the Broken Moon is a force to be reckoned with, and their ultimate goal is to bring about a new era of darkness and despair, with the shattered moon as their guiding light.
